Install in one tap

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open mbm01.life in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install on the banner at the bottom, or tap the ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open MBM '01 from your home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open mbm01.life in Safari (not inside WhatsApp; tap the link, then “Open in Safari”).
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The app is the same as the website, so it is always up to date. Remove it any time like any other app.
