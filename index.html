Getting there

Travel & stay in Jodhpur

The blue city awaits

Reaching Jodhpur

  • By air: Jodhpur Airport (JDH) is right in the city, a short drive from campus. Check current schedules for flights from your city. December is peak season, so book early.
  • By train: Jodhpur Junction has direct trains from Delhi, Jaipur, Ahmedabad, Mumbai and more.
  • By road: Good highways from Jaipur, Udaipur and Ahmedabad.
  • Airport & station pick-ups TBC

Where to stay

  • Group rate at a partner hotel TBC
  • Staying on your own? A base near Ratanada or Residency Road keeps you close to campus and the venues.
  • For a heritage-haveli stay, look near the Clock Tower in the old city.
  • Christmas week fills up fast, so hold a refundable booking now.

Good to know

  • Weather: sunny, pleasant days and cold nights (often near 10°C). Pack a proper jacket for the evening events.
  • Stay an extra day? Umaid Bhawan, Toorji ka Jhalra stepwell, Mandore Gardens and the Sardar Market shopping.
  • Must eat: mirchi bada, pyaaz kachori and makhaniya lassi. Just like old times.

Questions

FAQ

Who can attend?

Everyone from the MBM batch of 2001, across all branches. Whether spouses and kids can come will be confirmed with the registration form.

How much does it cost?

The per-head contribution will be shared once venues are locked. It will cover the event days and meals. Travel and stay are on your own unless a group package is announced.

Is there a dress code?

Casual for most of it. For the gala night there may be a theme, and there are rumours of a 90s theme. To be confirmed.

Can I attend only one day?

Yes. Tell us which days when you register so we can plan meals and seats.

Where will updates be posted?

On the batch WhatsApp group and on this page. Bookmark it.
